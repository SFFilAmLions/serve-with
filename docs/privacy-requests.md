# Manual privacy requests — administrator procedure (not deployed)

Contact: secretary@sffilamlions.org

1. Record receipt and the type of request without copying DOB, session tokens or credentials into tickets.
2. Verify requester identity using an appropriate secure process; an email from an unverified sender alone is not sufficient for destructive action.
3. Locate the account and connected records using authorized administrator access, limiting personnel with access.
4. Determine what can be removed and what must be retained for applicable recordkeeping, security or legal obligations. Avoid guessing a fixed retention period. Review foreign-key references, immutable acceptance history and DOB audit before modifying records.
5. For an approved deletion request, remove or minimize personal profile fields where practicable through a reviewed administrative operation, and record the reason for any retained data. Do not directly disable constraints or mutate append-only acceptance evidence without a legally reviewed retention/deletion strategy.
6. Inform the requester of the outcome and how to follow up. Escalate disputed or legally sensitive cases.

This is an operational checklist, **not** an implemented account-deletion feature or blanket authorization to destroy records. Do not export users' DOB or full profiles to outside event organizers. Only share the minimum details actually needed for the specific activity, using approved channels and retention limits.
